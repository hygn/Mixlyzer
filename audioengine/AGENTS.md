# AGENTS: audioengine/ (Mixlyzer)

The details of `audioengine/`: the two engines and the controls they share; the native engine is `audioengine/native/AGENTS.md`'s. The root `AGENTS.md`'s rules (run, layers, owners, change guidelines) apply here too; read it first.

## Map
- `audioengine/`: the player's audio engines. The native one is developed; the Python one is the fallback, kept as it is (its renderer design, `PCMFeeder`'s docs, is what the native one ported): deck 1 on one output, no MIDI, no mixer, no headphones or booth. Audio work goes to the native engine; the Python engine gets only what keeps deck 1 playing as the app expects.
  - `controls.py`: the deck's and the mixer's controls as both engines take them and report them: the deck's actions (`ACT_*`) and lamps (`LAMP_*`), a MIDI binding's flags (`BIND_*`) and events (`MIDI_EVENT_*`), the mixer's settings (`MIX_KEYS`) with their modes (`EQ_MODES`, `FADER_CURVES`), each output's last stage (`CLIPS`: hard / tanh / limiter, `LIMITER_DELAY_SEC`; the Python engine plays the master alone, the soft clip in place of the limiter), Sound Color FX (`COLOR_*`) and knob scales (`level_knob_to_db`, ...), `TEMPO_RANGES`, each engine's settings' ranges, and `DeckReport`; the numbers are `src/ipc.rs`'. The interface (`ui/`, `views/`) names these and `volume.py` only, never an engine's module (`tests/test_layers.py`); what it asks of the engines goes through `core.playback.player` (`native_engine_built`, ...).
  - `native/`: the default engine, Rust: `audioengine/native/AGENTS.md`.
  - `python/`: the fallback engine, PCMFeeder on a QAudioSink fed from the player's audio thread, with its time stretch (numba kernels), scratch reader and cursor poller. A device below 44.1 kHz (`controls.MIN_RATE`) gets no sink: nothing plays. Its buffer setting is one size with the time stretch on or off (`PCMFeeder.set_buffer_ms`: the sink opened again at the frame being heard where it changes; default 80 ms); another output device is another sink at the same format (`PCMFeeder.set_sink`). Its time stretch restarts a stream (about 40 ms of work) in the feed tick that seeks, and plays the original at unity while scrubbing, so under load it wants about 80 ms: it runs under the GIL.
